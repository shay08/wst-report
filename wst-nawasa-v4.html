Service Exclusion Finding™ — Gwanki, St. Mark
Water Signal Tracker™ · Infrastructure Visibility Gap Classification · June 2026
Exclusion — Not Variance

Classification: Service Exclusion Finding™. This is not a service restoration variance. Gwanki, St. Mark has zero pipe infrastructure. The community has never been connected to the water distribution network. There is no service to restore — because service was never extended.

Infrastructure Visibility Gap — Definition

An Infrastructure Visibility Gap is a community whose water access conditions — specifically, the complete absence of distribution infrastructure — are not reflected in official utility advisory or service records, because the community lies outside the service boundary. WST field observation documented Gwanki as this classification. Field photographs available for evidence reporting.

For CDB and FCDO: Gwanki represents a category of community excluded from the distribution network — not experiencing service disruption but experiencing infrastructure exclusion.
